# Research — validation & enrichment of the four redesign paths

Companion to [briefs.md](briefs.md). Read-only analysis: **no token in briefs.md
was changed.** WCAG maths computed with a local script (sRGB → relative
luminance, `(L1+.05)/(L2+.05)`, 2 dp); corpus = `ui-ux-pro-max` local database
at `/home/gouda/.agents/skills/ui-ux-pro-max`
(`python3 scripts/search.py "<query>" --domain <d> -n 5`, ANSI stripped).

Thresholds used: **4.5:1** normal text (incl. 17px and 12px mono caps), **3:1**
large text (≥24px, or ≥18.66px bold), UI borders, focus rings.
Surface-to-surface ratios are reported as _separation_ only — they are not a
WCAG criterion.

## Path D — "Homelab"

| Check               | Result                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Palette**         | text `#E6EBF1` **16.45**/15.43/14.32 · muted `#8C99A8` **6.79**/6.37/**5.92** · accent `#6E8BFF` **6.38**/5.99/**5.56** · ok `#4FD1A5` **10.32**/9.69/**8.99** · warn `#FFB454` **11.18**/10.49/**9.73**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

A bento grid separated only by a 1.22 border is close to invisible at low brightness. Line as an interactive boundary needs 3:1 → `#575E66`. Decorative tile edges are exempt, but grouping should not rest on 1.22 alone.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Typography**      | Locked Space Grotesk 600/700 + DM Sans 400/500 + JetBrains Mono 400 → **exact corpus match**: `"grotesk modern portfolio"` → **`Tech Startup` — Space Grotesk + DM Sans**, `family=DM+Sans:wght@400;500;700&family=Space+Grotesk:wght@400;500;600;700`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

Note `Portfolio/Personal` pattern is _Storytelling-Driven_, not bento — D is a deliberate departure.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **5 UX outcomes**   | **Hover vs Tap** (lift must not be the only affordance — corpus Animation) · **Reduced Motion** (staggered 40ms entrance must collapse to fade — corpus Animation) · **Truncation** (mono data/chips at 390px — corpus Content) · **Focus Appearance** (tile = link ⇒ whole tile needs a visible ring — corpus A11y) · **Target Size (Minimum)** (stack chips / ⌘K-less tiles ≥44px — corpus Touch)                                                                                                                              |
| **Motion tier**     | 200–280ms `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)` + 40ms stagger sits **between** corpus _Subtle_ (_Stagger List_ 250–350ms `power1.out`) and _Standard_ (_Scroll Reveal_ 400–600ms `power2.out`). Closest single preset: **Stagger List, Subtle**. Taking Standard would make the grid feel slower than the copy reads.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **3 anti-patterns** | 1) Shadows + 24px radii + glass blur (the style entry's defaults) — the fastest route to "empty AI SaaS page"; the brief's 1px borders and 16px radius are the corrective; 2) Colour-only status: `--ok`/`--warn` need a text or glyph marker, not hue alone (_Charts & Data_ rule: never rely on colour alone — and `--ok`/`--warn` differ for deuteranopes); 3) Placeholder artwork (stock photos, fake screenshot mockups) — the brief's "flat SVG, never stock photos" is the only viable route since no image assets exist. |

## Corpus disagreements (and who wins)

| # | Where the corpus contradicts the brief                                                                                                                                     | Verdict                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| - | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 | **B motion floor.** motion.csv's fastest tier is _Subtle_ = 150–200ms `power1.out`; nothing exists at 80–120ms, and no caret/typing preset exists (0 results twice).       | **Brief mostly wins** (snappy is the point), but keep the caret blink ≥1s and use 150ms as the floor for opacity/transform — 80ms reads as a glitch, and _Instant state changes (0ms)_ is a listed anti-pattern.                                                                          |
| 2 | **B typography.** Corpus's terminal pairing is mono+mono (`Terminal CLI Monospace`); the brief splits JetBrains Mono chrome + IBM Plex Sans prose.                         | **Brief wins.** Mono prose at 17px is the one thing the corpus pairing doesn't model; mono+sans (_Dashboard Data_) is the corpus's own answer for data-heavy interfaces.                                                                                                                  |
| 3 | **D tile separation.** `bento-box-grid` relies on soft shadows + hover scale 1.02 to separate 16px-radius tiles; the brief bans shadows and borders sit at 1.22.           | **Brief wins on the shadow ban** (it is what prevents SaaS-slop), **corpus wins on the missing cue**: add a non-shadow grouping cue — a 1px `--line` _plus_ a `--tile` step, or `#575E66` where the edge bounds a control. This is the one change worth making before mockups are judged. |
| 4 | **D vs `Portfolio/Personal` pattern.** The corpus says personal portfolios should be _Storytelling-Driven_, not a bento dashboard; it also prescribes parallax 3–5 layers. | **Brief wins.** The audit asks for an evidence layer, which bento delivers; parallax contradicts D's own reduced-motion story and _Excessive Motion_.                                                                                                                                     |
| 5 | **A typography.** No corpus pairing validates Roboto Condensed + Inter; nearest is Sports/Fitness (Barlow Condensed).                                                      | **Brief wins.** A's premise is brand continuity; Roboto Condensed 700 at 64px is a display setting, and Inter is the corpus's neutral body archetype. Recorded as a **corpus gap**, not a defect.                                                                                         |
| 6 | **B style.** No terminal/CLI style exists; nearest is `cyberpunk-ui`, whose effects the brief rejects.                                                                     | **Brief wins, but this is a real risk**: the "console" read must come from structure (status bar, paths, `man` sections), not from the cyberpunk effect vocabulary — the corpus has nothing to validate it against.                                                                       |
